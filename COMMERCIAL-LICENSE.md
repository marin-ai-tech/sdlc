## When you need a commercial license

- A business, or anyone working for one, uses sdlc on code that is not an open

- A consultancy, agency or contractor uses sdlc for client work that is not an
  open source project.
- You embed or redistribute sdlc, or a modified version of it, in a commercial
  product, or offer it as part of a hosted or managed service.
- A business keeps using sdlc after the 30-day evaluation.

You do not need one for noncommercial use, for open source projects as defined
in [sdlc Additional Permissions](LICENSES/sdlc-Additional-Permissions.md), or
during the evaluation period.

The royalty model and amounts are set in the commercial agreement. Typical

| Plan | Scope | Royalty |
|---|---|---|
| Team | up to an agreed number of developers using sdlc | per developer, per year |
| Organization | unlimited developers in one organization and its affiliates | annual fee |
| OEM / redistribution | sdlc embedded in, or distributed with, your product or service | share of revenue or per-unit royalty |

## How to obtain a license

Contact marin-ai technologies at https://github.com/marin-ai-tech (for a
first contact without confidential details you can also open an issue labelled
`commercial-license` in https://github.com/marin-ai-tech/sdlc). A commercial
license takes effect when a written agreement is signed.

After signing, record the agreement in the project so the logs and artifacts
that sdlc writes show the license that applies:

```bash
sdlc license set commercial --agreement <agreement-id> --licensee "<company>"
```
